Heat IllnessesHeat exhaustion: Your body gets too hot. You feel dizzy, weak, or sick.

Heatstroke: A dangerous medical emergency. Your body stops cooling itself. You need a doctor right away.

Sun and Dehydration Sunburn: Strong UV rays damage your skin. Use a strong sunscreen and wear a hat.

Dehydration: Hot weather makes you sweat out water. Drink fluids all day so you do not feel tired
