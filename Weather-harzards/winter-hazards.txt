## Winter hazards include dangerous conditions like freezing temperature, ice, and heavy snow that threaten safety and health.

# Three Major Hazards

Hypothermia and Frostbite: Extreme cold lowers core body temperature or freezes skin tissues.

Driving Dangers: Black ice, snow squalls, and freezing rain make roads slick
and visibility.

Heart Overexertion: Shoveling heavy snow raises the risk of heart attacks.
